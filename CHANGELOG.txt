Version 1.1.3
Created: 2026-08-19

Added:
- Added catalog, search, artwork, streaming, metadata, and download APIs for Aria.
- Added server-side YouTube Music album downloads and progress reporting.
- Added a Wi-Fi and Tailscale network watchdog for automatic recovery.
- Added a validated nightly yt-dlp updater with rollback and automatic repair retry.

- Repaired YouTube HTTP 403 failures caused by player and challenge changes.
- Made already-downloaded albums finish successfully instead of returning status 1.

Note: This was the first release recorded after version history was introduced.
